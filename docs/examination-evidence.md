# Examination evidence and written-answer teaching

Research dates: 26–27 October 2026. This extends the scientific topic research; it does not establish complete current syllabus coverage or examination readiness.

## Product requirement

Keep the topic-specific interactive models. Connect observation and explanation to examination work: interpret the instruction, select a principle, show derivation/substitution, give a complete written conclusion, diagnose an incomplete answer and review the student's own response. Do not substitute more controls for academic teaching, or substitute a question bank for conceptual understanding.

## Source criticism

- Reports establish what was examined and criticised in that historical session, not unchanged requirements for a current candidate.
- Paper 2's constant-temperature/internal-energy statement is taught with a fixed-quantity ideal-gas restriction, not as a rule for all materials or phase changes.
- Paper 3 contains a problematic alpha-particle charge-to-mass comparison and a questionable ultraviolet wavelength range; neither is copied as scientific authority. Its typeset/extracted SHM equation is also unreliable. Use the physically correct acceleration relation with squared angular frequency.
- Paper 4's displayed reciprocal-current equation makes the added constant have the units of reciprocal current, despite its prose calling that constant dimensionless. Do not teach the dimensional contradiction as a rule.
- Paper 4's eight readings, tangent at 33 ml and cited mark allocations belong to that question, not every practical. No general marking penalties or current assessment weights are inferred.

## Remaining authority gap

The public official specimen hub's A-Level button points to `#`, not a downloadable paper. The December 2024 syllabus mirror still exposes only partial content. Current full official syllabus/version applicability, assessment allocations and official question-level marking schemes remain unverified; see [the curriculum research](zimsec-curriculum-research.md).

Past-paper solutions must be labelled separately, record paper/session/question/page, preserve the verified data and conditions, cite the accessible source, and identify mirrors as mirrors. Paraphrase limited question material rather than republishing whole papers. Do not infer unseen diagrams, missing question parts or original numerical inputs from a report's final answers. Independently authored solutions are not official mark schemes; do not promise exact marks.
